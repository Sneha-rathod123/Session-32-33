TechNova Solutions is a fictional technology company founded in 2020.

The company provides software development, artificial intelligence,
cloud computing, and cybersecurity services.

The headquarters of TechNova Solutions is located in Pune, Maharashtra.

The company has 150 employees working in different departments.

The CEO of TechNova Solutions is Rahul Sharma.

The company's mission is to provide simple and innovative technology
solutions to businesses.

The company offers services to small businesses, startups, and large
organizations.
